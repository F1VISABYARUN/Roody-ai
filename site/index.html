What Roody does

🗣 Real voice, both ways

Talk naturally, hear replies in a warm human voice. Say "Roody stop" to cut it off — like a real friend.

👀 Reads the page with you

Articles, docs, code, courses — summon Roody and ask "what do you think of this?"

😄 A friend, not a bot

Jokes, honest opinions, encouragement. Pick a sidekick — a Minion might crash the conversation.

🌏 Speaks your language

🛡 Privacy first — by architecture, not promise

  • Loads nothing until you click. Roody only appears when you summon it.
  • Refuses personal pages. Banking, payments, email, chats, social media, government sites — Roody won't run there, even if you ask.
  • No servers, no tracking. Your conversations go straight from your browser to the AI provider. We never see them.
  • Read-only, always. Roody never edits, clicks, or types on any page.
