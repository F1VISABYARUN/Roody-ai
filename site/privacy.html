Roody Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Roody is a browser extension that acts as an on-demand AI coding assistant.

This policy explains exactly what data Roody touches and where it goes.

The short version

  • Roody loads nothing until you click its icon on a page.
  • Roody refuses to run on personal pages: banking, payments, email,

messaging, social media, government sites, and any page containing

password or payment-card fields.

  • Your conversations are never stored — not by us, not anywhere.
  • With your own API key, everything goes directly from your browser to the AI provider — we're not in the middle at all.
  • With an invite code, requests pass through our small relay server, which counts usage (number of calls, token counts) to enforce daily limits — it does not store the content of your pages or messages.
  • Creating a portal account is optional; if you do, we store only your email, display name, and invite code.

What Roody reads

When — and only when — you summon Roody on a page, it reads the visible text

of that page so the AI can help you with it. If you ask about something visual

(or tap the 📷 button), Roody captures a screenshot of the current tab and sends

that image with your message so the AI can see what you see. Screenshots are taken

only for a message you send, never in the background.

Where data goes

Your messages, short page excerpts, and any screenshot you attach are sent directly

from your browser to the AI service you selected in settings, using your API key:

  • Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com)
  • Anthropic Claude (api.anthropic.com)
  • xAI Grok (api.x.ai)

Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies.

If you use your own API key, Roody adds no middleman — requests go straight from your browser to the provider above.

If you use an invite code, requests are relayed through our server so we can apply your code's daily limit. The relay forwards your request to the AI provider and passes the reply back. It keeps usage metadata only — call counts, token counts, and estimated cost per code. It never stores or logs the content of your messages, your pages, or your screenshots.

Optional portal account & waitlist

You can use Roody with just an invite code and no account. If you choose to create a portal account (to see your usage at a glance), we store: your email address, an optional display name, your invite code, and hashed login tokens. If you join the waitlist, we store your email and optional name until we send you a code. We never sell or share these, and you can ask us to delete them at any time via the contact below.

What stays on your device

Stored only in your browser's local extension storage:

  • Your API keys
  • Your preferences (reply language, settings)
  • Roody's "notebook" — short snippets of pages where you talked to Roody,

so it can connect context across tabs

  • A usage/cost counter

You can view and erase the notebook and all settings at any time from the

extension's Settings page. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything.

What Roody never does

  • Never loads or reads pages in the background
  • Never runs on blocked personal-site categories, even if summoned
  • Never edits, clicks, types, or changes anything on any page
  • Never sells, shares, or transmits data to anyone besides the AI provider

you chose
